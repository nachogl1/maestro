    Throwaway prototype — Work Journal cards

    Not real code. It only shows how the decided pieces look together on one day's page.

    • The two plain lines at the top were accepted before cards existed; they stay plain.
    • Type in a card's text: everything before the first colon turns bold as you type. Remove the colon and the whole text is bold.
    • Click the time to change it; it stays bold.
    • Click the project chip to pick another open project, or none.
    • The source tag is fixed; a hand-made card says Typed.
    • Drag a card by ⠿ to move it among the notes; ✕ deletes it.
    • + Add entry (right of the formatting buttons) adds an empty card at the end with the current time.
    • Accept in Suggestions also adds a card at the end.
    • Free notes (the heading and the plain paragraphs) stay editable around the cards.
    • Long entries: the 11:30 card was pasted from a conference summary; every paragraph and list item stayed inside the card as its own line. It shows only its first sentence (up to the first ., ! or ? before a space, or the first line break) and See more.
    • See more opens the whole text in a read-only dialog with its time and project; Esc, ✕ or a click outside closes it.
    • Click into the long card's text: the whole text shows for editing. Click elsewhere and it shortens again. The stored text never changes.
