Let Claude read your conversations?

Two features send the text of your Claude conversations to Claude, through your own Claude CLI and your Claude allowance. Both stay off unless you switch them on.

Workshop
Shortly after start and then once a week, Claude reads the past week's conversations and writes a report on what to improve.
History summaries
For each conversation shown in History, Claude reads it and writes a name and a two-sentence summary. Again whenever the conversation changes.

You can change both at any time in Settings → Privacy.
