PRs pill (throwaway prototype)

Built only to react to; it is thrown away once you have. All data is made up. Click ⇄ PRs in the bar above. It replaces the old review-request badge, which jumped to the Git panel.

  • The pill: the total of every open pull request in your open projects. It is hidden when there is none. It is fetched when Maestro starts and every 5 minutes, so it has a count while closed.
  • Level 1: open projects with at least one open pull request, with their count; 👁 shows how many wait for your review. Projects with none (here scratch and notes-app) are hidden.
  • Level 2: that project's open pull requests: the ones waiting for your review first, marked 👁, then the rest, newest first. Drafts carry a small draft tag.
  • Level 3: a detail card inside the dropdown: title, number, author, draft or ready, checks, review state, created date, and an Open on GitHub button.
  • Back arrow and path as in the PROJECTS pill. Escape or a click outside closes it.
  • Loading: the last results are shown at once, with a small updating mark while they refresh; nothing is saved to disk. Because the total is fetched at start, a spinner shows only if the list opens before anything has loaded (the pill itself appears once that first fetch lands).
  • A failed project: billing-ui could not be loaded; it shows the error with Try again and the other projects stay. Its pull requests join the total once it loads.
